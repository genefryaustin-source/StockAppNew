Risk Disclosure

Comprehensive disclosure of investment, trading and technology risks.

Overview

This Risk Disclosure describes material risks associated with using AIQ Intellus and participating in financial markets. It is intended as a comprehensive disclosure framework requiring legal review before production.

Market Risks

  • Equity risk
  • ETF risk
  • Fixed income risk
  • Commodity risk
  • Currency risk
  • Digital asset risk
  • Liquidity risk
  • Volatility risk
  • Interest-rate risk
  • Inflation risk

Trading Risks

Options, futures, margin, leverage, short selling, forex and automated execution can produce substantial losses including losses exceeding invested capital where applicable.

Technology Risks

Cloud outages, exchange outages, internet interruptions, latency, cybersecurity incidents, software defects, AI errors, data corruption and third-party failures may affect availability, pricing, analytics and execution.

Model Risks

Historical performance, simulations, AI forecasts, optimization models and backtests are inherently uncertain and should not be relied upon as guarantees of future performance.

User Responsibilities

Users remain solely responsible for verifying information, supervising automation, validating orders and complying with applicable laws.
