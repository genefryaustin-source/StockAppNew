Cookie Policy

How AIQ Intellus uses cookies, browser storage, session identifiers, and related technologies.

Version: 1.0 Effective: August 1, 2026
This Cookie Policy explains how AIQ Intellus uses cookies, browser storage, session identifiers, and similar technologies. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

1. Overview

Conduro Ventures LLC, doing business as AIQ Intellus (“we,” “us,” or “our”), uses cookies and similar technologies to operate, secure, remember, measure, and improve our websites, applications, and related services. A cookie is a small data file stored by a browser. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, software-development-kit identifiers, device storage, pixels, and server-generated tokens.

2. Technologies We May Use

  • HTTP cookies: small values sent with web requests.
  • Session cookies: temporary cookies that generally expire when a browser session ends.
  • Persistent cookies: cookies retained until their expiration date or deletion.
  • Local storage: browser storage used for preferences, cached interface state, or approved session functions.
  • Session storage: browser storage intended to last for a single tab or browsing session.
  • Authentication and access tokens: values used to validate identity and authorization. Tokens may be held in memory, protected browser storage, or secure cookies depending on the product configuration.
  • Pixels and tags: small resources that may record that a page, email, or feature was accessed.
  • Device or application identifiers: identifiers used by mobile applications or security systems.

4. Strictly Necessary Technologies

Some technologies are required for the Services to function and cannot be disabled through an in-product preference center. Blocking them in your browser may prevent login, session continuity, security controls, portfolio tools, order interfaces, or other requested functionality.

5. Authentication, JWTs, and Sessions

AIQ Intellus may use secure session cookies or cryptographically signed tokens, including JSON Web Tokens, to represent an authenticated session. These values can be sensitive because possession may permit account access until expiration or revocation. Do not copy, transmit, or share session values. We may rotate, expire, revoke, or invalidate tokens for security.

Depending on configuration, the platform may also retain non-sensitive interface state in browser storage. We seek to avoid storing raw passwords or full third-party secrets in ordinary browser storage.

6. Third-Party Technologies

Service providers that support hosting, security, authentication, communications, payment processing, customer support, analytics, or embedded content may set or read technologies on our behalf. Their practices may also be governed by their privacy notices.

Connected brokerages, market-data providers, identity providers, or other external sites may use their own cookies when you visit or authorize them. We do not control technologies placed directly by an independent third party.

7. Your Choices

  • Browser settings: Most browsers let you view, delete, block, or limit cookies. Browser help pages explain available controls.
  • Device settings: Mobile operating systems may provide storage, analytics, or advertising controls.
  • Consent controls: Where legally required, we will provide a banner or preference mechanism for non-essential technologies.
  • Account preferences: Some analytics, notification, or personalization settings may be available in your account.
  • Do Not Track: Because there is no universally accepted technical standard, the Services may not respond to all legacy Do Not Track signals. Where required, we will recognize legally mandated browser-based opt-out signals.

8. Effects of Disabling Technologies

Deleting or blocking cookies may sign you out, reset preferences, interrupt integrations, or make features unavailable. You may need to repeat choices on each browser or device.

9. Retention

Cookie and storage duration varies by purpose. Session technologies ordinarily expire when the session ends or after inactivity. Persistent technologies remain until their configured expiration, deletion, or replacement. Related server logs may be retained separately under the Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when technologies, providers, features, or law change. We will post the revised version and update the effective date. Material changes will receive additional notice where required.

11. Contact

Questions or requests concerning cookies and similar technologies may be sent to privacy@aiqintellus.com.
