Financial-risk notice: Investing and trading can result in substantial losses, including loss of all invested capital and, for certain leveraged products or obligations, losses greater than the initial amount committed. Read this document before using any research or trading feature.

1. Informational and Technology Services Only

AIQ Intellus provides software, information, research, analytics, portfolio tools, workflow features, artificial-intelligence outputs, and interfaces to third-party services. Unless a separate written agreement expressly provides otherwise and all required registrations are in place, AIQ Intellus does not provide personalized investment advice or act as an investment adviser, broker-dealer, commodity trading advisor, commodity pool operator, fiduciary, financial planner, tax adviser, attorney, accountant, exchange, clearing agency, custodian, or transfer agent.

2. No Recommendation or Suitability Determination

Nothing in the Services—including rankings, ratings, scores, screens, alerts, forecasts, trade ideas, portfolio allocations, rebalancing outputs, sentiment indicators, risk metrics, research reports, or AI-generated language—is a recommendation to buy, sell, hold, short, hedge, or otherwise transact in any security, commodity, derivative, currency, digital asset, or strategy.

We do not know your complete financial circumstances, objectives, tax situation, liquidity needs, time horizon, legal restrictions, knowledge, experience, or tolerance for loss. An instrument or strategy displayed by the Services may be unsuitable for you.

3. Independent Review Required

You are solely responsible for conducting independent due diligence and determining whether a transaction is appropriate. Before acting, review official issuer disclosures, offering documents, exchange and broker rules, current market conditions, costs, tax consequences, liquidity, counterparty exposure, and legal restrictions. Consult appropriately licensed professionals when needed.

4. Risk of Loss

All investing and trading involves risk. Prices can change rapidly and unpredictably. You may lose some or all capital. Margin, options, short selling, futures, forex, leveraged products, and certain digital-asset transactions can create losses greater than the initial investment or require additional funding on short notice.

Past performance, historical simulation, backtesting, paper trading, and hypothetical results do not guarantee future performance.

5. Data May Be Delayed or Incorrect

Prices, quotes, fundamentals, economic data, news, estimates, corporate actions, classifications, and other information may be delayed, stale, adjusted, incomplete, duplicated, misclassified, or incorrect. Data from different providers may conflict. A displayed price may not be executable and may not reflect spread, depth, fees, slippage, or market impact.

6. Models, Scores, Forecasts, and Backtests

Models depend on assumptions, selected inputs, historical relationships, data quality, and implementation choices. Relationships may break down. Backtests can suffer from overfitting, look-ahead bias, survivorship bias, selection bias, data-snooping, unrealistic transaction-cost assumptions, and other limitations. Hypothetical performance does not represent actual trading.

7. Artificial-Intelligence Limitations

AI outputs can hallucinate facts, fabricate citations, misunderstand context, omit material risks, miscalculate, or rely on outdated information. Outputs may vary between runs and should not be treated as authoritative. Verify every material statement before reliance. See the AI Disclosure.

Order-entry or automation features do not guarantee transmission, receipt, acceptance, execution, price, timing, priority, cancellation, or settlement. Orders may be rejected, partially filled, filled at multiple prices, delayed, duplicated, or executed after a cancellation request. Third-party brokers, venues, custodians, and clearing firms control execution and account records. Their records govern if they conflict with a display in AIQ Intellus.

9. Market and Operational Events

Volatility, halts, limit states, illiquidity, market closures, corporate actions, news, exchange rules, outages, internet failure, cloud failure, API limits, provider maintenance, cybersecurity events, and extraordinary market conditions may affect access, data, alerts, and execution.

10. Asset-Class Considerations

  • Equities and ETFs: issuer, market, liquidity, dilution, concentration, sector, tracking-error, and corporate-action risk.
  • Options and derivatives: leverage, expiration, assignment, exercise, volatility, time decay, liquidity, model, and potentially unlimited-loss risk.
  • Short selling: potentially unlimited loss, borrow cost, buy-in, recall, margin, and dividend obligations.
  • Forex: leverage, rollover, dealer, jurisdiction, liquidity, intervention, and 24-hour market risk.
  • Digital assets: extreme volatility, custody, protocol, smart-contract, exchange, regulatory, fraud, liquidity, and irreversibility risk.
  • Fixed income: interest-rate, credit, call, reinvestment, liquidity, inflation, and default risk.
