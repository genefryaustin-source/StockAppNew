Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Conduro Ventures LLC, operating the AIQ Intellus platform (“AIQ Intellus,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, discloses, stores, and protects personal information when you use our websites, applications, APIs, research tools, portfolio features, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

Effective date: July 31, 2026 Privacy contact: privacy@aiqintellus.com
AIQ Intellus provides financial research, analytics, and portfolio intelligence tools. The Services do not provide individualized legal, tax, or investment advice, and this Privacy Policy does not alter any separate customer, subscription, data-provider, or brokerage agreement.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to personal information processed through the Services. It does not apply to third-party websites, market-data providers, brokerages, financial institutions, or other services that maintain their own privacy policies.

3. How We Collect Information

  • Directly from you, such as when you create an account, enter portfolio information, contact support, or configure preferences.
  • Automatically, through server logs, cookies or similar technologies, local storage, application telemetry, and security monitoring.
  • From connected services, such as brokerages, market-data providers, analytics providers, authentication providers, or other integrations you authorize.
  • From public and commercial sources, including financial markets, regulatory filings, company disclosures, and data vendors.

  • Provide, operate, maintain, personalize, and improve the Services.
  • Authenticate users and administer accounts, organizations, permissions, and subscriptions.
  • Generate portfolio analytics, rankings, research, alerts, reports, and AI-assisted insights.
  • Process user instructions and maintain portfolio, watchlist, and research records.
  • Monitor performance, diagnose errors, prevent fraud, enforce security, and protect users and the platform.
  • Communicate about service notices, support, security events, product changes, and administrative matters.
  • Comply with law, legal process, contractual duties, and regulatory obligations.
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims and enforce our agreements.

5. Legal Bases for Processing in the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

Where applicable, we process personal information under one or more of these legal bases:

  • Contract: processing necessary to provide requested Services or take steps at your request before entering a contract.
  • Legitimate interests: operating, securing, improving, and supporting the Services, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent: where you have provided consent, which you may withdraw where legally permitted.
  • Legal obligation: processing required to comply with applicable law.
  • Legal claims and vital interests: where processing is necessary to protect rights, safety, or legal interests.

The Services may use automated analytics and artificial intelligence to summarize information, rank securities, identify patterns, generate research content, or support risk and portfolio analysis.
